# Prompts to keep

Copy one into a chat inside the St. Joe Time Machine Project and fill in anything in [brackets].

## 1. Read the clipping

Here's a clipping. Transcribe it word for word and mark anything you can't read with [?]. Then tell me the newspaper, the date, and the 3 most interesting facts in it. Don't write a post yet.

## 2. Write the post

Write a Facebook post from this clipping in my style. Don't add any fact that isn't in the clipping. Mark anything I should double-check with [CHECK].

## 3. Make it a reel

Turn this story into a 45 to 60 second reel script. Give me a hook for the first 3 seconds, short lines I can read out loud, and what to show on screen for each line. Under 150 words, and end with a question for the comments.

## 4. Check my draft

Here's my draft and the clipping it came from. List anything in my draft the clipping doesn't support, any date or name that looks off, and anything a local reader might correct me on.

## 5. Answer a correction

Someone left this comment on my post: [paste comment]. Help me write a short, warm reply. If they're right, thank them and say I'll update the post. If it's unclear, ask them where they read it.

## 6. Find research leads

I'm researching [place, person, or event] in St. Joseph. Search the web for what's already written about it and list your sources with links. Then tell me which years and St. Joseph newspapers I should search on Newspapers.com, and what words to search for.

## 7. Plan my week

Here are the stories I have clippings for: [list them]. Which ones should be reels and which should be photo posts? Put them in an order for this week, strongest first.

## 8. Turn comments into the next post

Here are the comments on one of my posts: [paste them]. What questions, corrections and memories keep coming up? Suggest 2 follow-up posts or videos my readers are asking for, and tell me what I'd need to look up for each.
