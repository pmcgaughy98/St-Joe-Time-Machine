# What works on St. Joe Time Machine

Read from her public pages on October 20, 2026. Numbers grow over time; treat them as a snapshot.

## Platforms
| Platform | Followers | Posts read | Typical video |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 3,400 | 63 recent posts | 19,000 views |
| TikTok | 1,594 | 27 videos | 2,175 views |
| Instagram | 1 | 9 posts | about 150 views |

Facebook is the main audience. The same video gets about 4 to 7 times more views there than on TikTok.

## Videos beat text posts
Across 63 recent Facebook posts, the typical video got about 293 reactions and 19,000 views. The typical
text or photo post got about 19 reactions. Text-only crime stories got under 10 reactions even when the
story was strong. Part of the gap is that Facebook pushes videos to more people.

## Two formats that work
1. **Being there.** 15 to 25 seconds of her own phone footage walking the place, one line of on-screen
   text, music, no narration. The Glore tunnels video, her biggest, is this format.
2. **Narrated story.** 45 seconds to 2 minutes. Opens on a date or a place, short lines, real small
   numbers, one great quote, ends on a question. Felix's Grave is the model.

## What commenters do
- Share their own memories: working at the hospital in the 1970s, school field trips, parties at Krug Park.
- Work out old prices in today's money themselves. Give them the number.
- Correct details ("Before Blacksnake Hills it was Snake Hills").
- Ask for follow-ups: "Want to see what is beyond the block wall." "Does Atchison have a similar site?"
- Ask who narrates the videos.

## Known correction
The Blacksnake Hills video says Lewis and Clark passed by in 1805. The National Park Service has the
expedition reaching today's Buchanan County on July 4, 1804, and passing again in September 1806.
